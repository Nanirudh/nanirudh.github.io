Hi! I'm Anirudh, currently working at **Nvidia**, Bangalore as a Senior System Software Engineer. I'm part of the CUDA team, where I work on developing and enhancing features in the CUDA driver across different GPU platforms. I hold a Master's degree in Computer Science from IIT Kanpur and a Bachelor's degree in Computer Science from NIT Warangal.

Previously, I worked as a Backend Developer at Salesforce focusing on Application Security, and as a Full-Stack Developer at SAP Labs and VISA. During my Master's at IIT Kanpur, I explored deep learning and computational genomics through my thesis, [Representation Learning in Single-cell Multiomics Datasets](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s12859-026-06613-x) under the guidance of Prof. Hamim Zafar.

My interests lie in High Performance Computing, Machine Learning Systems, Operating Systems, Computer Architecture, Cloud Computing, Software Performance engineering and Competitive programming (Expert rating 1710 [Codeforces](https://codeforces.com/profile/annnnirudh)).

Skills: C, C++, CUDA, AWS, Java, Python, Pytorch, Machine Learning, GenAI tools and Software Engineering.
